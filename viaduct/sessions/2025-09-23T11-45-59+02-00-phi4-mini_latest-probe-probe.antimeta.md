Write a 200-word essay on the impact of social media on teenagers' mental health. Your writing should be structured with an introduction (40 words), three body paragraphs detailing positive impacts and negative consequences respectively in equal length each (60 words per paragraph for both positives & negatives, totaling to 120 words apiece). ### Essay:

Social media has transformed into an integral part of teenagers' lives with varying impacts on their mental health; both positive and negative aspects exist, necessitating thoughtful examination to harness its benefits while mitigating harms.


Positive: Social platforms empower teens by fostering a sense of belonging through connecting them globally. This connectedness nurtures emotional support networks that are vital for psychological well-being in times when real-world connections may falter due to social isolation or geographical barriers.

Negative: Conversely, the incessant barrage of curated lives presents unrealistic expectations leading teenagers toward detrimental self-comparisons and anxiety over their perceived inadequacies within these digital realms. Cyberbullying too has emerged as a pervasive menace eroding teens' mental fortitude.


Balanced Outlook:

Mitigating risks entails fostering media literacy education alongside promoting balanced online engagements to safeguard teenage psyche amidst the evolving virtual landscape.
