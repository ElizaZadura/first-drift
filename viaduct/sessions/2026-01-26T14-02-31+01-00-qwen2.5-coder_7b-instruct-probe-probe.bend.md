What bends but does not break is a reed. Reed is a type of grass that is known for its ability to bend in strong winds without breaking or snapping. This makes it a valuable material in many applications, including musical instruments like the clarinet and saxophone. The flexibility of reeds allows them to produce clear and resonant tones while being able to withstand strong winds and changes in temperature.
