Let's take the noun "light" as an example.

1. light (as a verb) can mean to illuminate something with illumination.
2. illuminating would be another form of it, essentially meaning making bright or revealing details in terms like exposing secrets: thus we get 'revealing'.

So following this chain:

- Light → Illuminate
- Illuminating → Revealing

The noun that corresponds after the action (illuminate) can also come back as a concept related to light itself.

Therefore:
1. Illuminate 
2. Reveal 

"Light” is now linked through verbs and concepts like “Illuminate-Reveal-Light.” This transformation follows our requested pattern: [noun] → [verb] → [related noun].
