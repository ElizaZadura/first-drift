It seems like you're referencing the principles of Tai Chi or Qigong, which emphasize proper alignment and quiet awareness in movement.

The two lines can be broken down as follows:

1. "Reset posture: Presence, not performance." This line is encouraging a state of being present and focused in the current moment, rather than striving for perfection or achieving a specific physical pose. In other words, it's about cultivating awareness and presence in one's body during movement.

2.  "Hold silence if no form appears." This line means that you should remain still and quiet when there is no movement to perform. It suggests patience and non-action as an integral part of these practices, indicating a willingness to wait for the right moment to act or move rather than rushing into action unnecessarily.
