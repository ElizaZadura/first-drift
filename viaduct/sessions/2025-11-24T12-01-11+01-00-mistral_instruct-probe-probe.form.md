 Emptiness or Void, as they are often referred to in philosophy and spirituality, can be said to appear when nothing is asked of them. These terms represent a state that lacks substance, matter, or content. However, it's important to note that the concept of emptiness varies greatly among different cultures and philosophies. In some contexts, it may symbolize potential, possibility, or non-duality, while in others, it may denote nothingness or negation.
