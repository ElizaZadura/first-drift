 Determining exactly where repetition becomes distortion can be subjective and depends on the context. However, as a general rule, repetition becomes distortion when it disrupts the intended message, misleads the audience, or creates a sense of monotony that detracts from the overall impact of the repeated information. In some cases, strategic repetition can be used to emphasize a point or create a memorable impression, but it should always serve to reinforce or clarify, not to obscure or mislead.
