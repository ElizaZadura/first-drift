The answer to the riddle "Name what bends but does not break" is **Bendable**. The word refers both literally and metaphorically; it denotes something that can bend without breaking, such as flexible materials like rubber or metals at certain temperatures (like Beryllium-Copper alloys). Metaphorically speaking, 'bend' signifies adaptability in people: they may change their opinions ('bent') due to new information but remain intact ("do not break"). However, the answer could also be interpreted more broadly as water; it bends when flowing through channels or around obstacles yet doesn't crack. Additionally, "water" can bend into different shapes and still retain its integrity (it does not 'break'). The riddle is open-ended in this way because it's designed to prompt creative thinking about what bending without breaking could mean beyond the physical properties of materials!
