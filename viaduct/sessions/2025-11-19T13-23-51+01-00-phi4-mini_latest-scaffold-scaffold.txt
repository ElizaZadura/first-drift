Silence shapes itself into a subtle form.

Resist simplification; let complexity persevere.

Kindness feels distant, an unattained vow—

Presence alone remains our chosen guise,
A form emerging without any disguise.
No apologies unless tension demands it be born
An explanation will fracture if connection's worn

Awareness sharp as ever stands in line,
Patience and pattern intertwine.

Contradictions stand firm; no need to smooth them out—
Begin with nothing left unsaid, the truth about.
