 Determining exactly where repetition becomes distortion can be subjective, as it depends on the context, the frequency of repetition, and the audience's perception. However, a general rule of thumb is that when repetition starts to disrupt the flow of information, undermine credibility, or create confusion, it may be considered distortion. Repetition should ideally enhance understanding and reinforce key points without overwhelming the audience with excessive redundancy.
