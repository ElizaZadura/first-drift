 In the world of yoga and mindfulness, "Reset Posture" is a term often used to describe a neutral alignment of the body that helps reduce strain on muscles and joints while promoting optimal physical function. This posture can serve as a foundation for various practices like meditation or asana (yoga poses). Here's a step-by-step guide to finding your reset posture:

1. Stand tall with feet hip-distance apart, toes pointing forward.
2. Engage your abdominal muscles by gently drawing your navel towards your spine (this is called "bracing" or "activating the core").
3. Roll your shoulders down and back, away from your ears.
4. Let your arms relax at your sides with palms facing forward.
5. Tuck your chin slightly in to lengthen the back of your neck.
6. Close your eyes if it feels comfortable for you, or keep them open with a soft gaze downwards.
7. Breathe deeply and evenly through your nose, feeling your chest and abdomen rise and fall naturally as you inhale and exhale.
8. Hold this posture for a few moments, allowing your body to find stillness and ease.
