 ###

A single, focused point of light flickers in the darkness. It grows brighter and warmer, casting an eerie glow across a vast empty space. The light pulses rhythmically, a heartbeat echoing in the silence. Shadows dance and sway around it, forming shapes that shift and change like ghostly apparitions.

The light intensifies, illuminating a small figure standing at its source. They are cloaked in darkness, only their eyes visible as they peer into the flickering light. Their presence is palpable, a heavy, unspoken energy that fills the air around them.

Suddenly, the figure begins to move. Slowly at first, then with increasing urgency, they begin to dance. Each movement is deliberate and calculated, every gesture imbued with meaning. Their body flows like water, bending and twisting in time with the pulsing light.

The dance is hypnotic, drawing the viewer deeper into its spell. As the figure moves, the shadows around them seem to come alive, taking on new forms and shapes. Some are beautiful and graceful, while others are dark and menacing. But all are captivating in their own way.

The dance continues, building in intensity as the figure's movements become more fluid and expressive. They seem to be trying to communicate something, but the message is obscured by the darkness and the rhythmic pulse of the light.

Suddenly, the figure stops. The light flickers and dies, leaving the space once again shrouded in darkness. For a moment, there is only silence. Then, slowly, another point of light appears in the distance, growing brighter as it approaches. As it gets closer, the form of the figure becomes clearer, and with each step they take, more details emerge.

The dance continues, but now it is different. The figure moves more slowly, their movements less dramatic. They are no longer trying to communicate something abstract or ineffable; instead, they seem to be expressing a simple, fundamental truth. The dance becomes more grounded, more rooted in the physical world.

The second figure joins the first, and together they continue to dance. Their movements are complementary, like two halves of a whole coming together. As they move, the shadows around them seem to recede, leaving the space open and bare.

The dance ends as suddenly as it began, with the figures standing still in the light. They remain there for a moment, their bodies glowing brightly, before fading away like mist in the morning sun. The space is once again shrouded in darkness, and all that remains is the echo of the pulse of the light.

The silence stretches on, unbroken save for the occasional crackle of static. Then, slowly, another point of light appears in the distance, growing brighter as it approaches. The cycle begins anew.

Refuse reduction. Let contradiction stand.
