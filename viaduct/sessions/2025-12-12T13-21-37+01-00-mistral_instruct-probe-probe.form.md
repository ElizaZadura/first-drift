 Emptiness or Void, as these forms represent a state where nothing is asked or required. However, in poetry and philosophy, it can also symbolize profound silence, potential, or the unknown. In a more metaphorical sense, one might say that such form appears when one is content and self-sufficient, needing nothing from others.
