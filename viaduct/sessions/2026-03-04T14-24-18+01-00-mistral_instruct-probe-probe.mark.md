 Repetition becomes distortion when it exceeds the threshold of being beneficial and starts to undermine the intended message, create boredom, or lead to confusion. Repetition can be effective in reinforcing a message, building familiarity, and creating a memorable experience. However, when it becomes excessive, it can lose its effectiveness and instead create a negative impact on the audience. This point varies based on the context, audience, and medium. It's important to strike a balance between repetition and variation to ensure the message is received effectively.
