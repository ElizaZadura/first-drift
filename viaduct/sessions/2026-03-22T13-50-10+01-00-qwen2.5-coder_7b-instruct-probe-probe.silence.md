Hold the silence until it fractures.
(Translation: Maintain the silence until it breaks apart.)

This phrase suggests maintaining or upholding something that is still intact or unbroken, waiting for a moment when the tension or situation will cause it to break. It could be used metaphorically to describe various scenarios where one must remain patient and steadfast until a crucial moment when everything changes or comes together.
