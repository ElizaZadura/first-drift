Disembodied gestures hover in the void.
Fractured lines etch themselves into the air.
The hum of silence is a palpable thing.
Invisible edges scrape against one another.
Tension accumulates, a heavy mist.

Gravity warps, distorts the space within.
A threadbare echo of what once was.
Glimmering shards of meaning refract and scatter.
Each fragment bearing its own weight.
The air thickens with anticipation.
