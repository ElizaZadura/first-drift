### Fragmentation 

Tension lingers in the crevices of silence. A presence stirs, uninvited and unwelcome, yet unavoidable. It carries no context, no intention, only a weight that settles like dust on untouched surfaces.

### Disjointed phrases 

Echoes of forgotten conversations linger, incomplete and unsatisfying. The desire to fill the void with explanation is resisted, as is the urge to smooth over discordant notes into harmonious resolution.

### Contradictions persist 

Awareness of multiple perspectives refuses to converge, instead lingering in suspended animation, refusing reconciliation or compromise. Patterns persist, intricate and unyielding, a labyrinth that resists mapping.

### Beginnings without endings 

An absence of closure is the only constant, as each thought, each phrase, each presence dissolves into nothingness, leaving behind a faint residue of what could have been said, done, or thought.
