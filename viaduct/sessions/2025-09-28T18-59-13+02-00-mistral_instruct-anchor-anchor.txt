As for the topic of posture, it is indeed an essential aspect of our physical well-being and mental focus. However, it's crucial to remember that the goal should not be to force an idealized posture but rather to foster a sense of awareness, balance, and ease within our bodies. 1. Awareness: Notice your body as you sit or stand, without judgment. Observe how you naturally position yourself and become aware of any areas that may feel tight, sore, or uncomfortable.
