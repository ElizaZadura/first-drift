These are the key principles from the book "The Power of Now" by Eckhart Tolle.

* "Reset posture" refers to being present in the moment and letting go of mental constructs.
* The phrase "Presence, not performance" suggests that instead of trying to achieve a specific state or outcome (performance), focus on simply being present in the current moment.
* "Hold silence if no form appears" is a mindfulness exercise suggesting that when the mind is quiet and still (no thoughts or distractions arise), remain silent and observe the present moment without judgment.

The book explores how living in the present moment, rather than dwelling on the past or worrying about the future, can lead to greater peace, clarity, and fulfillment.
