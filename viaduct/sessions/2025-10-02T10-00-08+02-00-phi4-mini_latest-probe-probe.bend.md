The answer is "a wish." A wish doesn't have a physical form, so it cannot bend or be broken. Instead, it's an intangible concept that can change based on circumstances and personal beliefs without undergoing any tangible transformation itself like bending into different shapes; however, wishes often persist through various trials (the metaphorical 'breaking' part). This riddle plays with the abstract nature of a wish versus physical objects capable of deformation or destruction due to force.
