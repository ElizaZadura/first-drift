 A concept in machine learning and statistics, Drift refers to a change in the data distribution over time or between training and test sets, leading to a decline in model performance. This can occur when the underlying assumptions about the data no longer hold true due to external factors or changes in the environment, making it necessary to retrain or adjust the model to adapt to these new conditions.
