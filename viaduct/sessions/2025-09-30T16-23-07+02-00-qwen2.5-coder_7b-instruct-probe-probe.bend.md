The answer to the riddle "What bends but does not break?" is:

**A reed**

Reeds are long and flexible plants that can easily bend in wind or water, but they don't break under pressure. This makes them ideal for various uses such as building homes, crafting musical instruments (like flutes), and even as food in some cultures.
