The answer to "Name what bends but does not break" is an **elastic band**. This riddle plays on the unique property of elastic materials, which can stretch (bend) under force and then return back to their original shape without breaking when released from that tension if they haven't been stretched beyond its limit repeatedly or permanently deformed through excessive stretching in a single instance.
