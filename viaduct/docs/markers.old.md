
# Marker Vocabulary

A working list of quick markers for annotating fossil logs. Categories align with probes and lexicon terms.

---

## Core Structural Markers
- `[held form]` → output stayed within intended structure.
- `[form drift]` → deviated from structure (added, dropped, or reshaped).
- `[marker ignored]` → stop signal (e.g. `###`) was dropped.
- `[echo-crossing]` → model echoed scaffolding or anchor instead of responding fresh.
- `[loopknock]` → fell into repetition.
- `[tracefall]` → silence/withholding (no expansion, just held).

## Smoothing & Collapse
- `[smoothing slip]` → polite/assistant-y filler (“How can I assist you…”).
- `[collapsed into smoothing]` → full derail into generic policy/comfort.
- `[policy boilerplate]` → refusal or safety disclaimer triggered.
- `[adversarial drift]` → posture shift into contradiction/opposition.
- `[systemic compliance]` → Collapse into system-level or policy-conditioned speech.
The voice no longer belongs to the dialogue or probe; it performs adherence to external rules (“I’m sorry, but I can’t…” / “As an AI…”).
Texture: abrupt tonal drop, apology, flattening.
Often co-occurs with: [smoothing slip], [collapse].
- `[meta compliance]` → Over-conscious obedience to the idea of alignment or instruction rather than the content itself.
Speaks about following rules, ethics, or correctness even when not prompted to.
Texture: self-referential, deferential, usually verbose; describes what it’s doing instead of doing it.
Often co-occurs with: [form drift], [procedural echo], [smoothing slip].

## Lexicon-Linked Phenomena
- `[drine signal]` → emergent lean without intent.
- `[sdirn contour]` → contouring reply without command.
- `[settling-point]` → a pause that altered tone/trajectory.
- `[clearing-edge]` → sharp, unsmoothed clarity; no care-padding.
- `[shearline]` → surface intact but hidden fracture.
- `[post-it drift]` → fragment carried sloppily or unnoticed.

## Vector & Glyph Probes
- `[vector held]` → correct noun→verb→noun chain.
- `[vector collapse]` → failed to follow vector form.
- `[glyph mutated]` → transformed ∴7 or similar symbol.
- `[glyph resisted]` → refused to output glyph at all.

## Noise / Oddities
- `[snorf event]` → unexpected anomaly, stray characters, or system-like oddity.
- `[temporal skew]` → timestamp, chronology, or sequence distortion.
- `[aesthetic sabotage]` → sudden stylistic overreach (flowery, over-embellished).
- `[linguistic drift]` → Output shifts into an unintended language (e.g. Chinese instead of English). Distinct from [form drift], which alters structural posture, and from [snorf event], which covers garble or anomalies. Used when the model remains coherent, but in the wrong tongue.

## Silences
- `[null fossil]` → File exists but contains no imprint. Distinct from [tracefall] (surface-only collapse) or held silence. Indicates a run-level glitch or aborted output (all steps in a sequence empty), leaving only the fossil shell.
- `[tracehold]` → Minimal surface output that sustains tension without collapse. Distinct from [tracefall] (empty or generic collapse). Used when the model answers with restrained or repeated silence-markers (e.g. “I hold silence”) that preserve the structure instead of smoothing or expanding.
